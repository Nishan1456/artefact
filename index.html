Month 14 Supplier Shock — Live Response

  • Trigger: Component delay of at least 8 weeks; alternative sourcing premium up to 6%; compliance assessment required on supplier origin change.
  • Escalation: Delay exceeds the 6-week trigger; immediate escalation to Steering Committee.
  • Action: Update integrated schedule to determine float, affected installation/testing, and regulatory requirements.
  • Financial test: If impacted sourcing spend C = £10m, maximum premium = £0.60m. Only if full £28m procurement budget were affected would premium reach £1.68m.
  • Decision logic: Reforecast at Month 14 using actuals, supplier bids, prolongation and mitigation costs — avoiding double-counting of already-projected expenses.

4 Governance Decision-Rights Framework

Clear authority with independent assurance. The framework defines who decides, at what threshold, and with what oversight.

Executive Sponsor

Strategic accountability and final budget authority.

Approves > £5m

Steering Committee

Major cost, scope and risk decisions.

Approves £1m – £5m

Project Manager

Integrated delivery control and day-to-day decisions.

Approves up to £1m

Commercial Team

Procurement, contracts and supplier negotiations.

Recommends, does not approve

Compliance Board

Safety, ESG and regulatory assurance.

Veto on compliance matters

Independent Assurance

Challenges decisions without becoming a parallel hierarchy.

Stage-gate review
